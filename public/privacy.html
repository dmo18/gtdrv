Rustwater privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

Rustwater does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. The game works entirely on your device.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress, scores and settings (for example the steering style you chose, and sound settings) in the local storage of the app or browser on your device. This stays on your device. We never receive it, and we cannot see it. Clearing the app data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It has no in-app purchases.
  • It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • It makes no network requests while you play. After the first load of the web version, or from the very first launch of the Android app, it works offline.
  • It contains no code from advertising or social networks and does not set cookies.

Web version hosting

If you play the web version, the website host (GitHub Pages) delivers the files and, like any web host, may keep ordinary server logs such as your IP address. We do not receive or use those logs. GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that hosting.

Children

Rustwater contains cartoon style violence and blood and is meant for adults (17 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
